# CIPHER-FUSION: Important Limitations & Scope Disclaimers

## Mandatory System Limitations

1. **Synthetic Data Focus**:
   This MVP prototype is designed specifically for authorized hackathon demonstration using **synthetic or anonymized demo datasets**. It must not be populated with unencrypted real PII or live sensitive citizen data outside secure law enforcement air-gapped workstations.

2. **No External Live API Connectivity**:
   CIPHER-FUSION operates strictly offline. It does **not** connect to live bank core servers, telecom carrier BTS networks, OSINT lookups, police databases (CFCFRMS / NCRP), or payment gateways.

3. **No Dynamic Malware Execution**:
   Android APK analysis is strictly limited to **passive text/JSON metadata extraction** (package name, requested permissions, signing certificate SHA-256, embedded C2 domains). Uploaded APKs or binaries are **never** executed, compiled, or dynamically run.

4. **No Proof of Criminal Identity or Guilt**:
   CIPHER-FUSION produces deterministic risk indicators and graph relationships based solely on supplied digital artifacts. It does **not** make conclusive claims regarding individual criminal intent, guilt, or identity.

5. **No Automatic Legal or Financial Seizure**:
   The platform does not automatically issue freezing orders, warrants, or legal notices. All recommendations in the Golden-Hour Action Queue are designated as *"Recommended Verification Actions"* for review by human investigators.

6. **Forensic Summary Standard**:
   Reports generated by CIPHER-FUSION bear the mandatory legal disclaimer:
   > *"Forensic-ready investigative summary generated from supplied artifacts. Findings require review and verification by authorized personnel. This report does not independently establish identity, intent, guilt, or legal admissibility."*
